**Protomate · Software Engineer Team Lead · January 2023–February 2024**

## Overview

Protomate was a Thai startup developing connected, AI-enabled motorcycle helmets for rider safety. The company has since ceased operations. I led the software team responsible for the integrated platform spanning the **helmet, mobile app, and remote control**.

## My role

- Led the **seven-person software engineering team** implementing the helmet platform, **Flutter mobile app**, and **nRF-based remote control**.
- Managed requirements and timelines, aligning software priorities with the mechanical and electrical engineering leads.
- Designed device-to-app connectivity using **Bluetooth Classic and Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)**.
- Implemented **Secure Boot** and integrated the mobile app and remote control with **custom Android firmware**.

## System integration

The project required coordinating software across three connected components: the helmet's Android firmware, the Flutter app, and the nRF remote control. My work covered the connectivity between those components as well as integration with the hardware teams' roadmap.

**Technologies:** Flutter, custom Android firmware, Bluetooth Classic, BLE, nRF, Secure Boot.
