**Creative Machines Lab, Columbia University · Sep 2026 – Present**

## Goal

Build an agent that can beat human players at foosball, starting from policies trained in simulation.

## My contribution

- Trained reinforcement learning agents to play foosball in simulation using **NVIDIA Newton and PhysX**.
- Built an **AlphaStar-style league training system** combining **PPO, self-play, and Policy-Space Response Oracles (PSRO)** with a **Nash meta-solver**, so agents stay robust to a diverse population of opponent strategies.
- Investigating **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models** as policies for the agent.

**Tools:** NVIDIA Newton, PhysX, PPO, PSRO.
