**RoboCup@Home 2022 · Open Platform League · World first runner-up**

## The robot

I helped build **Walkie**, a household service robot, as part of the team competing in RoboCup@Home. Eleven teams from seven countries qualified for the final.

## My contribution

- Implemented pre-labeling models for **open-vocabulary object detection** using **ViLD (Vision and Language Knowledge Distillation)**.
- Assisted the perception sub-team with **person-tracking and pose-estimation algorithms**.

## Team result

The team finished as **world first runner-up** in the 2022 Open Platform League. My work focused on perception within the larger robot system.
